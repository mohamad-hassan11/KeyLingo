# Vocabulary practice and review

Open **Language practice** (`/en/practice`, also `/nl` or `/ar`) and choose a practice
language, A1–B2 level and category. The 84-entry AI-reviewed pilot has 28 entries per
language; C1/C2 show an honest unavailable state. Levels are approximate, and no
human linguistic validation is claimed. Arabic casual content is informal MSA.

Open an exercise, begin typing, then select an annotated word or **Translate the
full text**. The dialog pauses timing. Choose an independent translation language,
save a word, and close or press Escape to return to typing. A manual pause remains
in effect until explicitly resumed. Free lookup requires an exact surface or lemma;
unknown words are not guessed. Wiktionary opens only if you choose its link.

Open **My vocabulary** to search, reveal, retype or remove saved items. Saving the
same entry/sense/target stores one item; saving another target stores a separate
snapshot. Words retain their source context/version even if future content changes.

Preferences, ended-session progress and saved vocabulary use separate local envelopes.
Vocabulary export and last-good-backup recovery are available. Saving failures show
a warning and preserve memory data for export/app navigation; failed saves cannot
survive reload. Concurrent tabs are supported with revision retries but local storage
is not transactional synchronization. Import/reset controls arrive in Phase 5.
